Acme Manufacturing — Company Snapshot

Industry: Automotive components (stamping, CNC machining, assembly, and
painted subassemblies for tier-1 automotive suppliers)

Sites: Lyon, France (primary plant, ~950 employees) and Stuttgart, Germany
(secondary plant, ~550 employees)

Founded: 1987. Family-owned until a private equity minority investment in
2019, which came with an expectation of a formal digital transformation plan
within three years — a deadline that has now passed without one being
delivered.

Product lines: stamped chassis brackets, CNC-machined transmission
components, welded subframes, and painted trim assemblies. Roughly 60% of
revenue comes from three long-term contracts with major automotive OEMs, all
of which include on-time-delivery penalty clauses.

Recent context: A major customer audit last year flagged the plant's lack of
real-time production visibility as a risk to the relationship, though no
formal corrective action plan has been filed yet. Two customers have also
begun asking for Scope 1/2 emissions data as part of their own supplier
sustainability reporting requirements — a request Acme currently cannot
fully answer.

Workforce: Average tenure on the production floor exceeds ten years. Roughly
15% of production staff are within three years of retirement eligibility,
concentrated among the most senior machine operators.

Current systems: A 12-year-old ERP system (no MES layer), spreadsheet-based
maintenance tracking, and no centralized data warehouse. Digital initiatives
to date have been plant-level pilots rather than company-wide programs.
